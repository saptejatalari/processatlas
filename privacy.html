The Instagram publishing app

The app "Postings" (with "Process Atlas Publisher") is a private tool. It publishes content to the Instagram account @processatlas, which belongs to the person named above, using Meta's official API. It is not offered to other users, and nobody else logs in to it.

The app handles only data about that one account:

  • the access token for @processatlas, kept in Windows Credential Manager on one private computer
  • the account's user ID, username, follower count and post count
  • its own posts, their links, and total figures for each post, such as views, reach, likes, comments, saves and shares

The app does not read the text of comments or direct messages, and it does not collect names or profiles of followers or visitors. The figures are totals, stored on that computer to review how posts perform. They are not sold or shared. Tokens are deleted when they are no longer needed, and the stored data is kept only while the account is run with this tool.
