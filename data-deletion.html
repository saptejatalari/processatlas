Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The app "Postings" is a private publishing tool for the Instagram account @processatlas. No one else logs in to it, and it stores no personal data about followers or visitors. See the privacy policy for details.

To ask us to delete your data

  1. Send a direct message to @processatlas on Instagram, or write to the address below.
  2. Say that you want your data deleted, and give your Instagram username if you have one.
  3. We delete any data we hold about you and confirm by the same route, within one month.

Remove the app's access

If you ever connected this app to an Instagram or Facebook account, you can remove it at any time. On Instagram, open Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites. On Facebook, open Settings → Apps and websites. Then choose the app and remove it.

Your data on Instagram

Comments and messages you send on Instagram are held by Meta. You can delete them in Instagram or through Instagram's privacy tools.
